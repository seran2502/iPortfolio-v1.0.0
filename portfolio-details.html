Spacely

A warehouse space-sharing mobile app that helps businesses find suitable storage space and helps warehouse owners share unused capacity.

Project typePersonal project
PlatformMobile app
My roleUI/UX Designer
ToolsFigma, Figma Make, Google Stitch

Project overview

Spacely is designed around two user groups: Seekers, who need warehouse space for a selected size and period, and Hosts, who have available warehouse capacity to list and rent out. The experience brings discovery, space information, booking requests, communication, and booking management into one place.

Problem statement

Businesses often need flexible warehouse space for short-term storage, while warehouse owners may have unused capacity during off-season periods. Finding a suitable space, understanding its cost, and coordinating a booking can take time. Spacely aims to make this process clearer and more convenient for both sides.

Design goals

Easy discovery

Help seekers find spaces using location, size, price, and availability.

Clear information

Present warehouse photos, facilities, capacity, pricing, and details clearly.

Smoother booking

Make request, host approval, payment, and booking status easy to follow.

Key features

For Seekers

  • Search, recommended, and nearby warehouse listings
  • Warehouse details, photos, facilities, and ratings
  • Cost estimate based on area and rental duration
  • Move-in date selection and booking request
  • Booking status, chat, and booking history

For Hosts

  • Create, edit, and manage warehouse listings
  • Live and draft listing states
  • View incoming booking requests
  • Accept or decline requests
  • Manage accepted bookings, chat, and history

My design process

01 · Understand

Identify the needs of warehouse seekers and space owners and define the core problem.

02 · Structure

Organize app content, roles, navigation, and the main user journeys.

03 · Design

Create wireframes and mobile UI screens with reusable components and auto layout.

04 · Prototype

Connect key screens and interactions to demonstrate the end-to-end experience.

Tools used

  • Figma
  • Figma Make
  • Google Stitch
  • ChatGPT

What I learned

This project helped me practice designing for two different user roles, structuring end-to-end flows, building reusable components with Auto Layout, and connecting screens through interactive prototyping. It also helped me understand how clear booking states and timely information can reduce confusion in a multi-step service.
